# CONTRIBUTING.md

## Purpose

Describe how humans and AI collaborate on this repository.

## Human Responsibilities

-   Decide content.
-   Review technical accuracy.
-   Approve significant changes.

## AI Responsibilities

-   Generate drafts.
-   Maintain formatting.
-   Update navigation.
-   Suggest improvements.
-   Automate publishing tasks.

## Guiding Principles

-   Accuracy over speed.
-   Practical over theoretical.
-   Preserve engineering intent.
-   Keep examples production-oriented.
